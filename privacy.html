Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

TesterUtils is designed as an offline-first desktop application. Test content you enter into the app is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded by TesterUtils to a cloud service.

Information collection

TesterUtils does not require a user account and does not include application analytics or telemetry that collects your test content. The app does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information entered into its testing tools.

Local data

Settings, recipes, favorites, history, and other application data may be stored locally on your device so the application can provide its features. Exported files are saved only when you choose to create them.

Microsoft Store services

TesterUtils may communicate with Microsoft Store services to determine licensing or entitlement status and to support the optional TesterUtils Pro purchase. Those Microsoft services are operated by Microsoft and are subject to Microsoft's applicable privacy terms. TesterUtils does not process payment card information itself.

Clipboard and files

TesterUtils accesses clipboard content only when you explicitly use a feature that requires it. File access is initiated by your actions, such as opening or exporting a file.

Third-party sharing

TesterUtils does not sell personal information and does not intentionally share your test content with third parties.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when TesterUtils features or privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact method provided on the TesterUtils Support page.
